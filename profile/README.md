### 👋 Who we are

We were started by a group of CUHK computer science graduates. Our project
teams cover the full delivery cycle — project management, business analysis,
system analysis, UI/UX design, development, and quality assurance. We build
custom web and mobile systems for the public sector, NGOs, education and local
businesses.

Most of our work is delivered under client agreements, so this org is mostly
private. What's public here is how we work: our shared TypeScript libraries,
our coding standards, and the reusable CI workflows every project builds on.

### 🛠️ What we do

- **Build** — web applications, mobile apps, internal tools and back-office systems, from first workshop to launch.
- **Run** — hosting, monitoring and ongoing support once a system is live.
- **Advise** — helping teams pick an architecture and plan a build they can maintain afterwards.
